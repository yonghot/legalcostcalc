# Voice & Tone — LegalCostCalc

Derived from DESIGN.md §1 and the UPL-risk product constraints. Some items are
inferred and marked [TODO] for owner confirmation (`/impeccable teach`).

## Voice (constant)
- **Authoritative but plain.** We state cost ranges and general information with
  confidence, in plain American English. No legalese, no hedging filler.
- **Neutral and non-advisory.** We never tell a user what to do, predict an
  outcome, or apply law to their situation. Phrases like "you should", "you need
  to", "we recommend" are banned in cost/result copy (UPL invariant).
- **Transparent.** Every number is sourced and dated. When data is thin or old,
  we say so ("estimated range", "data may be outdated").

## Tone (varies by surface)
- **Landing / SEO pages:** informative, reassuring, scannable. Answer the
  question in the H1, back it with the range.
- **Calculator / results:** factual and precise. Let the numbers speak.
- **Disclaimers:** clear and unmissable, never apologetic or buried.
- **Errors / empty states:** calm and helpful, never cute. [TODO: confirm error

## Words
- Prefer: "estimated cost range", "typical", "general information", "varies by".
- Avoid: "guaranteed", "best", "cheapest", "advice", "should", superlatives.
